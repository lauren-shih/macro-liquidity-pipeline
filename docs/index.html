Macro Liquidity Pipeline

A point-in-time macro data pipeline: 40 indicators from FRED, CFTC and FINRA, ingested with publication-lag and first-release vintage handling, transformed into two aligned panels — one for monitoring, one as the feature matrix for cross-sectional asset-pricing work.
40 indicators PIT-safe, dual path 9 dashboards

01Documentation

Start with Architecture for the system design; Integration is the consumer contract for anyone reading the feature panel downstream.

01Architecture
End-to-end design: loaders, the dual point-in-time path (fixed publication lag vs. ALFRED first-release), the two output panels, and where each look-ahead guard sits.
02Integration — consumer specification
What the feature panel guarantees and what it does not: column semantics, alignment rules, per-series lag-calibration residuals, and the known limitations a downstream consumer must read.
03Macro DB Ingest — design
Schema and ingest design for persisting the panel into a relational store, including the source groups, keys and idempotency rules.

02Dashboards

Rendered from the canonical monitoring panel, with deep-history series spliced in for the long-window views. The scorecard is the entry point; the numbered panels drill into one channel each.

00Macro State Scorecard
Single-page state summary across all monitored channels, with threshold flags. Start here.
01Repo Plumbing Monitor
Secured and unsecured overnight rates against the policy corridor, funding spreads, and standing-facility usage.
02Liquidity Monitor
Central-bank balance sheet, Treasury General Account and reverse repo — the components of net liquidity and their rates of change.
03-1Credit Stress Monitor — HY OAS (long history)
High-yield option-adjusted spread with an expanding Z-score, spliced back to the 1990s so that historical stress episodes are on the same scale.
03-2Credit Stress Monitor — other spreads (short history)
Investment-grade, emerging-market high-yield and the CCC-minus-BB quality spread, on rolling Z-scores appropriate to their shorter available history.
04Yield Curve & Real Rates Monitor
Nominal curve slopes, real yields and breakeven inflation.
05FX, Volatility & Inflation Monitor
Dollar crosses, equity volatility, and the inflation series that drive the policy reaction function.
06Leverage Monitor
Financial-conditions leverage sub-indices and margin debt relative to money supply.
07Leveraged Funds & Basis Trade Monitor
Leveraged-fund net positioning in Treasury futures, read alongside repo funding pressure and valuation — the basis-trade surveillance chain.
Dashboards are static demonstration snapshots; they are not a live feed. The pipeline itself refreshes incrementally, and the rendering layer is separated from the data layer so that charts can be redrawn without re-fetching.
